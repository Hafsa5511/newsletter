Book Quest
App

2025  ·  App Design  ·  Figma

A gamified reading app that transforms books into epic adventures. Book Quest makes reading addictive by turning every book into a quest with XP rewards, achievements, and social challenges.

Featuring a dreamy dark purple aesthetic with a starry night theme, pixel art avatars, and smooth animations that make reading feel magical and rewarding.

What it does

Key Features

Quest System

Books become quests with XP rewards based on difficulty and length.

Reading Streaks

Daily reading habits earn bonus rewards and maintain streaks.

Social Challenges

Race friends to finish books and climb weekly and monthly leaderboards.

Customizable Avatars

Unlock and customize pixel art characters as you level up.

Community Reviews

Share reviews, see what friends are reading, and discover new books.

Achievement System

Unlock badges for milestones like "First Quest," "Speed Reader," and "100 Books."

Progress Tracking

Chapter-by-chapter tracking with reading time and pages logged.

Curated Discovery

Find your next read through genre filters, trending quests, and personalized recommendations.
